### Changelog

Built on Gadgetbridge **2026.11.0** (upstream: https://codeberg.org/Freeyourgadget/Gadgetbridge)

#### 2026.11.0-kepler (versionCode 273)

For watch firmware 3.3.4; runs with older firmware as before.

* Based on Gadgetbridge 2026.11.0 (was 0.95.1); upstream now uses date version numbers. Full list in the upstream changelog
* Kepler: Longer battery life while connected to the phone: the app asks for a low-power connection after connecting (watch firmware 3.3.4 then sets its own slow interval)
* Kepler: Dismissing a notification on the watch also dismisses it on the phone (watch firmware 3.3.4)
* Kepler: Larger Bluetooth packets, so the watch's notification history and diagnostics are no longer cut short (watch firmware 3.3.4)
* Kepler: Choose the alarm tone and the hourly chime tone; picking one plays it on the watch (watch firmware 3.3.2)
* Kepler: The watch settings show only what the watch's firmware has; nothing greyed out
* Kepler: Settings changed while the watch is away are sent on the next connect
* Kepler: Device icon in the new Kepler colours
* Kepler: The setup after connecting runs once per connection, so settings are no longer sent twice
* Kepler: The call popup on the watch keeps working on the new Gadgetbridge base
* Kepler: Default alarms are five different times and days, all off, instead of five identical 06:30 entries (new alarm lists; alarms already set up stay as they are)
* Kepler: Watch modes list shows plain names (Timer, Find Phone, Counter 1, ...), matching the manual
* Kepler: German texts rewritten (du-form, "Telefon" throughout, button and screen names as in the manual)
* Kepler: Clearer wording for the image screen, the second time zone, the "modes left out" message and the pair-again notification
* Kepler: Tone names without the Casio model name
