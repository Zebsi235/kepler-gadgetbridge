### Changelog

Built on Gadgetbridge **2026.11.0** (upstream: https://codeberg.org/Freeyourgadget/Gadgetbridge)

#### 2026.11.0-kepler (versionCode 273)

For watch firmware 3.3.4; runs with older firmware as before.

* Based on Gadgetbridge 2026.11.0 (was 0.95.1); upstream now uses date version numbers. New look for the alarm list, charts, dashboard and device search, and a one-time "What's new" screen after the update. Full list in the upstream changelog
* Kepler: Longer battery life while connected: the app asks the phone for a low-power connection after connecting, and watch firmware 3.3.4 then asks for its own slow interval
* Kepler: Dismissing a notification on the watch (hold the right button on the Notifications screen) also dismisses it on the phone (watch firmware 3.3.4)
* Kepler: Choose the alarm tone and the hourly chime tone; picking one plays it on the watch (watch firmware 3.3.0)
* Kepler: The watch settings show only what the watch's firmware has; nothing greyed out
* Kepler: Settings changed while the watch is away are sent on the next connect
* Kepler: The setup after connecting runs once per connection, so settings are no longer sent twice
* Kepler: The call popup on the watch keeps working on the new Gadgetbridge base
* Kepler: Default alarms are five different times and days, all off, instead of five identical 06:30 entries (new alarm lists; alarms already set up stay as they are)
* Kepler: Watch modes list shows plain names (Timer, Find Phone, Counter 1, ...), matching the manual
* Kepler: German texts rewritten (du-form, "Telefon" and "Batterie" throughout, button and screen names as in the manual)
* Kepler: Clearer wording for the image screen, the second time zone, the "modes left out" message and the pair-again notification
* Kepler: Tone names without another watch's model name
* Kepler: Device icon in the new Kepler colours

#### 0.95.1-kepler-2 (versionCode 267)

For watch firmware 3.3.2; runs with older firmware as before.

* Kepler: Tapping "Found" when the watch alarm or timer rings on the phone stops it on the watch too (watch firmware 3.3.2)
* Kepler: Second time zone: pick a zone and the clock face shows its time and a three-letter name in the seconds corner, with daylight saving changes sent ahead (watch firmware 3.3.2)
* Kepler: The app connects to the watch again as soon as the phone's Bluetooth is switched back on, without opening it (can be switched off in the watch settings)
* Kepler: A one-time alarm that rings while the alarm screen is open is no longer armed again, so it does not ring a second time or again the next day

#### 0.95.1-kepler-1 (versionCode 266)

* Based on Gadgetbridge 0.95.1 (was 0.94.0): refreshed design for charts, workout details and the device list, fixed reconnect when the system restarts the service, fixed popup menus and more; full list in the upstream changelog
* Kepler: Watches are called "Kepler Watch"; the app also finds watches that advertise "Kepler" or "Kepler Watch". A watch added earlier is renamed once, a name you gave it is kept
* Kepler: Every transfer to the watch (image, alarms, sound settings, settings, weather, notifications, time) shows its progress when it takes longer than a moment, says when an image or the alarms are on the watch, and a failed one can be tried again from its notification
* Kepler: Image upload shows a progress bar; the Send button cannot start a second upload; a dropped connection is reported and the image goes again on reconnect
* Kepler: Image gallery: recently sent images and presets (Kepler logo), shown pixel for pixel as on the watch
* Kepler: Photos taken in portrait are shown upright
* Kepler: The phone stops ringing when the watch alarm or timer is stopped (watch firmware 3.3.2), and by itself after 60 seconds with older firmware; a phone search started from the watch keeps ringing
* Kepler: Correct time on the watch in time zones beyond 9 hours from UTC, such as Sydney, Auckland and Hawaii (watch firmware 3.3.2)
* Kepler: The notification mark on the watch face is set again on every reconnect, so it goes off when the phone's notifications were cleared while the watch was away
* Kepler: Notification list and mark stay right when Android restarts the notification access, and no longer break a reconnect when a notification arrives at the same moment
* Kepler: Repeating alarms keep ringing on watches with firmware before 3.1
* Kepler: An SMS that is never reported read no longer keeps the notification mark lit (it ends after a day)
* Kepler: "Pair again" offers no longer replace each other with two watches
* Kepler: No crash when Bluetooth permission is taken away while the watch is connected
* Music control: a failing media key fallback no longer crashes the app
* Kepler: The app is now a release build (not debuggable), signed with the same key, so it installs over the previous version and keeps all settings and pairings
* Kepler: All other Kepler features carried over unchanged: watch modes, sounds and alarms, music, find phone, notifications
